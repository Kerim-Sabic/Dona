## 📖 STUDY TECHNIQUES RESEARCH

### **Evidence-Based Methods (2024-2025)**

#### 1. **Active Recall**
- **Effectiveness:** Highest-rated study method
- **Implementation:** Actively quiz yourself instead of re-reading
- **Best Practices:**
  - Create flashcards
  - Practice questions
  - Teach concepts to others
- **Results:** Strengthens memory retrieval pathways

---

#### 2. **Spaced Repetition**
- **Effectiveness:** Scientifically proven for medical students
- **Optimal Schedule:**
  - Day 1: Initial learning
  - Day 3: First review (1-2 days later)
  - Day 7: Second review
  - Day 14: Third review
  - Continue doubling intervals
- **Critical:** Don't delay first review more than 1 day
- **Tools:** Anki, Knowt, Gizmo use this algorithm

---

#### 3. **Pomodoro Technique**
- **Effectiveness:** Increases efficiency by up to 40%
- **Structure:**
  - 25 min focused work
  - 5 min break
  - After 4 cycles: 15-30 min long break
- **Variations:** 7-3-2-1 method (2025 trend)
- **Integration:** Combine with active recall during sessions

---

#### 4. **Hybrid Method: SmartPomodoro** (2025 Trend)
- **Concept:** 7-3-2-1 inside Pomodoro cycles
- **Structure:**
  - 7 min study
  - 3 min review (active recall)
  - 2 min break
  - 1 min preview next topic
- **Best for:** Science & exam prep
